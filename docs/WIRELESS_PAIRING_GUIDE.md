## English

### Method A: One-Click Convert from USB to Wireless (Recommended)

This is the easiest and fastest method:
1. Connect your Android phone to your PC using a USB cable.
2. Accept the **"Always allow from this computer"** USB debugging prompt on your phone.
3. Open **AirLink Studio**. Your phone will appear on the Dashboard.
4. Ensure your phone is connected to the same Wi-Fi network or mobile hotspot as your PC.
5. Click the **"Convert to Wireless"** button on the USB Connection card.
6. AirLink Studio will automatically detect your phone's Wi-Fi IP, enable TCP mode on port 5555, and connect wirelessly.
7. You can now unplug the USB cable!

---

### Method B: Android 11+ Wireless Debugging Pairing (No Cable Needed)

1. Make sure your PC and Android phone are on the **same Wi-Fi network or PC Hotspot**.
2. On your phone, go to **Settings &rarr; System &rarr; Developer Options**.
3. Scroll down to **Wireless Debugging** and toggle it **ON**.
4. Tap directly on the text **"Wireless Debugging"** to enter its settings screen.
5. Tap **"Pair device with pairing code"**.
6. A dialog will appear displaying:
   - **Wi-Fi pairing code** (6 digits, e.g. `123456`)
   - **IP address & Port** (e.g. `192.168.1.50:37451` — note the pairing port!)
7. In AirLink Studio, click **"Pair with 6-Digit Code"** on the Wireless Wi-Fi card.
8. Enter the IP, Pairing Port, and 6-digit Code into the modal and click **"Pair & Connect"**.
9. Your device is now paired and will be remembered for 1-click reconnection!

## Hinglish (Roman Hindi)

### Tareeqa A: USB se Wireless mein 1-Click Convert Karein (Sabse Aasan)

1. Apne phone ko USB cable se computer se jodein.
2. Phone screen par aane wale **"Always allow from this computer"** prompt ko allow karein.
3. **AirLink Studio** open karein. Aapka phone Dashboard par dikhai dega.
4. Dhyaan dein ki aapka phone aur laptop **same Wi-Fi ya Mobile Hotspot** se jude hon.
5. USB Connection card par **"Convert to Wireless"** button dabayein.
6. AirLink Studio apne aap aapke phone ka Wi-Fi IP dhundkar wireless mode shuru kar dega.
7. Ab aap USB cable nikaal sakte hain aur bina taar ke screen mirror aur control kar sakte hain!

---

### Tareeqa B: Android 11+ Wireless Debugging (Bina Cable ke Pair Karein)

1. Phone aur PC ko **same Wi-Fi network ya laptop hotspot** se connect karein.
2. Phone ke **Settings &rarr; System &rarr; Developer Options** mein jayein.
3. **Wireless Debugging** option ko ON karein.
4. **"Wireless Debugging"** ke upar tap karein taaki uski settings khulein.
5. **"Pair device with pairing code"** par tap karein.
6. Phone screen par ek pop-up aayega jisme dikhega:
   - **6-digit pairing code** (jaise `482910`)
   - **IP address aur Port** (jaise `192.168.1.100:39812`)
7. AirLink Studio mein Wireless card par **"Pair with 6-Digit Code"** button dabayein.
8. Phone par dikhne wala IP, Port aur 6-digit code daalein aur **"Pair & Connect"** par click karein.
9. Phone pair ho jayega aur Recent Devices mein save ho jayega taaki agli baar ek click mein connect ho sake!
